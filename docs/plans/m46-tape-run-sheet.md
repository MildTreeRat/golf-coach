# M46's tape session: the run sheet

**Tier: TARGET until the session runs, then a record of what was asked.** Written 2026-11-12 by M46 P2,
for P3 of [the plan](m46-impact-drill.md), the first of M46's two sessions that only you can run.
Everything the desk phases P4 to P13 need comes out of it, so the aim is that it happens **once**: read it
through before you start, and keep it beside you.

**What you need**: STRIKEPRO labels of all three types (driver, iron and hybrid), a ruler or calipers, a
printer, the clubs in step 4, somewhere to hit (a net is enough, and no launch monitor is needed), the
iPhone, and a pen and paper for notes.

**What you leave behind**: every photo in `data/raw/strike_tape/<date>/` on the desk, untouched, and a
`labels.txt` beside them filled in from [the template](#labelstxt). The P3 session then checks the folder
against [the last table](#what-each-item-is-for) and asks for anything missing.

**Why the details matter.** P13 measures the reader's error by comparing each on-club reading with the
same mark on the peeled label, matched strike by strike in the order you hit them. A miss, a retake or a
skipped photo that is not written down shifts every match after it on that label, and a label stuck in its
slot the wrong way round mirrors every mark on it. Those are the notes this sheet asks for.

---

## Before you hit

1. **Print the truth sheet and check the print.**
   - Open [`spikes/strike-tape/truth-sheet.svg`](../../spikes/strike-tape/truth-sheet.svg) in a browser
     and print it at **100% ("Actual size")**, never "Fit to page", with the default margins, in colour if
     you can. Print **three**: one for the fresh labels (step 2) and two for the used ones (six slots
     each). Print one more for every six labels past twelve.
   - With the ruler, measure on each print: the **scale bar** at the foot, end tick to end tick, must be
     99.5 to 100.5 mm; and the **two left corner squares**, from the top edge of the top one to the bottom
     edge of the bottom one, must be 249 to 251 mm. If either is off, look for a scaling setting in the
     print dialog and reprint.
   - Write both numbers in `labels.txt` (`print:`), and number each sheet in its `Sheet` field. Mark the
     one for step 2 `FRESH`.

2. **Measure each label type, and photograph one of each flat.**
   - Take one **unused** label of each type and stick it on the `FRESH` sheet: the driver label in slot 1,
     the iron label in slot 2, the hybrid label in slot 3. Square it to its slot, centred, with the edge
     that goes along the face's top line toward the slot's number. Hold it by its edges and press it down
     through its backing paper rather than with a fingertip, in case pressure marks it.
   - Measure each with the ruler or calipers (the grid under it is a ruler too), to the half millimetre:
     - **width**, toe end to heel end, and **height**, top edge to bottom edge;
     - **its shape**: if it is a rounded rectangle, the **corner radius** (how far from the corner the
       straight edge starts, read off the grid). If it is any other shape (tapered like an iron face,
       say), sketch it on paper with each straight edge's length and where its corners sit, in
       millimetres from the label's left and top edges, and photograph the sketch;
     - **any printing**: a logo, text, a centre mark, a scale or a grid, with where it is and its colour.
       Note the label's own colour, and whether you can see the grid through it.
   - Photograph the `FRESH` sheet as step 7 says.
   - If a label is **taller than 60 mm**, stop and note it, since it would cover the band below its slot.
     If one is **wider than 90 mm**, its labels go in the left-hand slots only (1, 3 and 5), with the slot to
     their right left empty. Anything up to 90 mm wide goes in its slot, centred, even when it overhangs.

3. **Answer two questions**, in `labels.txt`:
   - Which label goes on a **fairway wood**: driver, hybrid, iron, or none?
   - Which label goes on the **putter**: driver, hybrid, iron, or none?

   The rest is assumed, and you correct it in `labels.txt` if it is wrong: the driver label on the
   driver, the hybrid label on a hybrid, and the iron label on every iron (long, mid and short) and every
   wedge.

## At the net

4. **Hit and photograph, one club at a time.**
   - **The clubs**, at least: the **driver** with a driver label; a **hybrid** with a hybrid label (or a
     fairway wood with one, if you have no hybrid); **one long or mid iron**; and **a wedge**. Between them,
     all three label types must be hit, because the reader's error is measured per type. If there is
     time, a second label on the driver and on the hybrid gives P13 twice the strikes to measure them by.
   - **Finish one label before starting the next**, so each label's photos are one unbroken run. Number
     the labels L1, L2, … in the order you apply them, and note each one's club and whether you hit it
     right- or left-handed.
   - **Apply the label** where the pack says, or centred on the face. Its centre is taken as the face's
     centre, so put it where you mean the middle to be.
   - **Do not photograph the clean label.** The drill will never have that photo, so the first photo of a
     label is the one after its first strike.
   - **After every strike, one photo**, with the iPhone's own Camera app: Photo mode, **Live Photo off**,
     flash off, 1×, no zoom.
     - Hold the club upright, grip at the top and head at the bottom (**the shaft up**), with the face
       toward the phone.
     - **Fill the frame with the head**, with the hosel and the bottom of the shaft in view and the whole
       label inside the frame. The hosel is how the reader tells heel from toe.
     - Hold the phone **square** to the face (parallel to it), and tap the label to focus.
     - Even light, with no bright reflection across the label: if there is glare, move rather than tilt.
   - **A bad photo** (blurred, or the label cut off): take another before the next strike, keep both, and
     note the second as a **retake** of the first.
   - **A strike that misses the label** (no new mark): still take the photo, and note it as a **miss**.
   - **Stop a label** once it has 6 to 10 marks, or sooner if it lifts or tears. Note how many strikes it
     took, misses included.

5. **Skip a photo, twice.** Twice in the session, on two different labels, hit two balls before taking
   the photo, so that photo shows **two** new marks. Note its file name as **two new**. Never do this on a
   label's first photo: two marks on a fresh label is a different case, and it is not the one being
   tested. If the two marks land on top of each other, note that too.

6. **A few photos off square.** On each label type, for two strikes, take the square photo first, then
   **one more** with the phone turned 20 to 30° off square (about a third of the way from square to
   side-on): once to the left or right, and once up or down. Note each as an **extra**, with its direction.
   The extras test the reader at an angle without breaking the label's run.

## Afterwards

7. **Peel each label onto the sheet, then photograph the sheets.**
   - After a label's last photo, peel it off by its edges. **Keep the side the camera saw facing you, and
     do not turn it.** Lay it in the next empty slot as it looked in the photos: the edge that ran along
     the top line toward the slot's number, and the toe on the same side it was in the photos (**left for
     a right-handed club, right for a left-handed one**). Smooth it down through its backing paper.
   - Fill in the slot's band: club, hand, label number, and strikes.
   - **Photograph each sheet straight on**, the `FRESH` one included: the sheet flat on a table, the phone
     held level above it (parallel to the sheet), **all four black corner squares** in the frame with a
     little margin, and no shadow of the phone or your hand across it. Take **two** of each sheet.

8. **Copy every photo to the desk, untouched.** Strikes, retakes, misses, extras, the `FRESH` sheet and
   the truth sheets all go into `data/raw/strike_tape/<YYYY-MM-DD>/`, the session's date, keeping their
   file names. HEIC is fine. Do not edit, crop, rotate or rename any of them.
   - AirDrop to the Mac keeps the originals. Over USB to Windows, first set the iPhone's
     **Settings → Apps → Photos → Transfer to Mac or PC → Keep Originals**, or the phone converts them to
     JPEG on the way.
   - The sketches from step 2, if any, go in too.

9. **Write `labels.txt`** in the same folder, from your notes and the template below.

make. File names are as they arrived (`IMG_0123.HEIC`), and a range is the first and the last of a
label's photos, extras and retakes included.

## What each item is for

So that nothing is skipped as optional. Each item feeds a phase on the desk, by its number in
[the plan](m46-impact-drill.md).

| Item | Step | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| The print's two measurements | 1 | P13: the sheet is rectified to its drawn millimetres, so a print off scale would scale every truth mark |
| Each label type's width, height, shape and corner radius | 2 | P4: `tape_labels.json`, each number cross-checked against the `FRESH` photo within 1 mm |
| The printing, colour and see-through notes | 2 | P9 (finding the label against the face) and P11 (the clean label's colour) |
| The `FRESH` sheet's photos | 2, 7 | P4's cross-check, and P9 and P11 |
| The fairway wood and putter answers, and any correction | 3 | P4: the `category_labels` map, and which of C9's tables ship |
| What a strike looks like | 4 | P3's finding, then P11 (darker, lighter or coating gone decides how a mark is segmented) |
| Every strike photo, as a run per label | 4 | P4's inventory, P8 to P12's tuning, and P13's gate |
| Club, hand and face finish per label | 4 | P4's inventory, P9 (the label against that face), P10 (hand cross-checks orientation) |
| Misses and retakes | 4 | P4's inventory: each photo's index on its label, so P13 matches the right truth mark |
| The two photos with two new marks | 5 | P4's inventory (a photo before was skipped), and P12, whose gate is that both read as two marks |
| The off-square extras | 6 | P9 (the aspect tolerance) and P13 (the error at an angle) |
| The peeled labels, in order, in their slots | 7 | P13: `truth.json`, every mark a label took |
| Each slot's band (club, hand, label, strikes) | 7 | P13: which label is in which slot, which way its toe points, and how many marks to expect |
| The truth sheets' photos | 7 | P13 |
| `labels.txt` | 9 | P3's check, then P4 |
